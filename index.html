AN OBJECTIVE YOUR BUSINESS DEFINES

Make the tradeoffs explicit.

What should the agent team optimize? Adjust the priorities to see how an illustrative objective changes.

J = αE + βC + γT

E = error · C = operating cost · T = completion time
Minimize J, subject to your quality and approval constraints.

J = 0.50E + 0.30C + 0.20T

The strongest penalty is on errors: prioritize more verification and review.

Concept only. Weights are normalized; terms would also need comparable scales. This does not run or train an AI system.
